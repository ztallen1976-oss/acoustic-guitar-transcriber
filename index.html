    How to use this, step by stepTakes 2 minutes to read
    1. Get a recording. Any MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC of solo acoustic guitar. A phone recording works. Clean is better: one guitar, no singing, little background noise. Trim it to just the song.
    2. Drop it in. Drag the file onto the dashed box in 1 Recording, or click the box and pick the file. A progress bar shows while it listens. Roughly 10 seconds of waiting for every minute of music. Keep this tab open while it works.
    3. Tell it what you know (optional, but it helps a lot). In 2 Details and requests, type the song title, and in the big box write anything you know in plain words, like capo 2, DADGAD, 6/8, the intro is played freely. Press Apply requests. Little tags appear showing what it changed.
    4. Give it your rough tab (optional). In 3 Your rough tab, paste tab you already have, even if it's messy or only part of the song. It uses your string and fret choices wherever they match what it hears. You can also load a .txt or MusicXML file, or (on the Claude version) a photo of handwritten tab.
    5. Check it by ear. Press the round ▶ button. Use Recording to hear your file, Tab to hear what was written down, or Both to hear them together. When they match, the tab is right. The teal line shows where you are. Click anywhere on the tab or the waveform to jump there. Speed 50% helps with fast parts. The space bar plays and pauses.
    6. Fix what sounds wrong with the settings on the left. The tab redraws instantly every time you change one, so try things freely; nothing is lost. See the fixes below.
    7. Save it. Scroll to Download. The .musicxml file opens in MuseScore (free, musescore.org) or Guitar Pro, where you get standard notation and tab together and can edit any note and print. The .txt file is plain text tab you can paste anywhere.

    Quick fixes

    The count feels twice too fast or too slow
    Press ½× or 2× next to Tempo.
    Beat 1 lands in the wrong place, or the bars feel off
    Type the real tempo in the Tempo box if you know it. Try a different time signature (a waltz is 3/4, a lilting "one-two-three, four-five-six" feel is 6/8).
    The player speeds up and slows down
    Set Timing to Free timing (rubato).
    Wrong notes everywhere, or frets that make no sense
    The tuning is probably wrong. Pick it in Tuning, or choose Custom… and type it low string to high, like C G D G B D. If there's a capo, set Capo on fret.
    Too many stray notes
    Slide How many notes to keep toward Fewer.
    Quiet notes are missing
    Slide it toward More.
    Shapes are in the wrong place on the neck
    Lower Highest fret to 12 to keep it near the nut, or paste a few bars of your own tab in 3 Your rough tab to show it how you play it.
    x marks where there are no slaps or taps
    Untick Percussive hits.
    Triplets come out as odd 16ths
    Set Note grid to Triplets.
    The bass and melody stems look cluttered
    Untick Bass and melody as separate voices.

    Good to know

    • This gives you a strong first draft, not a finished score. Expect to tidy some note lengths and fingerings by ear, especially in fast or busy passages.
    • Harp and artificial harmonics can't be told apart from regular harmonics, so they show as <12>-style harmonics or fretted notes. Check those spots.
    • Your settings are remembered on this computer for next time.
    • The recording never leaves your computer. All the listening happens in this browser tab.
